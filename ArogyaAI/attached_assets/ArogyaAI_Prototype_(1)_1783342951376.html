Judge Trust Note — what's real vs. simulated in this prototype

Real, running live in your browser: the stockout formula, the cluster rule, doctor-attendance correlation, the hospital capability-vs-distance matcher, the real OpenStreetMap of Indore with actual hospital locations, the Hindi voice agent (speech synthesis) and Hindi speech-to-text input (on supported browsers) — all computing against seeded PHC data. Simulated for demo reliability: the "AI extraction" step replays rule-based keyword classification rather than a live Gemini call, and phone/SMS delivery is shown as on-screen notification cards, not real dispatch. Both are honestly labelled throughout.
